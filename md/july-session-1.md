### Resources

[**The Science Of Visual Data Communication: What Works**](The Science of Visual Data Communication- What Works.pdf): Academic paper on the perceptual science underlying good practice in data visualization.

[**Financial Times Visual Vocabulary**](https://github.com/ft-interactive/chart-doctor/tree/master/visual-vocabulary): A very thorough classification of chart types by visualization task.

[**ColorBrewer**](https://colorbrewer2.org/): A key resource for selecting color palettes for data visualization.

[**Viridis Color Palette Generator**Links to an external site.](https://waldyrious.net/viridis-palette-generator/): The viridis palettes are a good alternative or addition to ColorBrewer's sequential palettes. They are particularly suitable if you need a reversed palette running from darker to lighter colors for contrast against a dark background.

[**Viz Palette**](https://projects.susielu.com/viz-palette): Examine how a color palette will look like for various types of chart, including colorblindness simulation.

[**How to pick more beautiful colors for your data visualizations**](https://blog.datawrapper.de/beautifulcolors/): Lisa Charlotte Muth of Datawrapper with a nuanced consideration of color choices.
