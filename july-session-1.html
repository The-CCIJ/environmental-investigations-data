Resources

The Science Of Visual Data Communication: What Works: Academic paper on the perceptual science underlying good practice in data visualization.

Financial Times Visual Vocabulary: A very thorough classification of chart types by visualization task.

ColorBrewer: A key resource for selecting color palettes for data visualization.

Viridis Color Palette Generator: The viridis palettes are a good alternative or addition to ColorBrewer’s sequential palettes. They are particularly suitable if you need a reversed palette running from darker to lighter colors for contrast against a dark background.

Viz Palette: Examine how a color palette will look like for various types of chart, including colorblindness simulation.

How to pick more beautiful colors for your data visualizations: Lisa Charlotte Muth of Datawrapper with a nuanced consideration of color choices.
